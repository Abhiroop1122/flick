# Code of Conduct

Flick is a small project and we'd like it to be a friendly one.

## Our standards

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the people who use Flick.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language,
publishing someone's private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you
represent the project. Report problems privately to the maintainer through their GitHub
profile. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
